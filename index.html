Your 4x2 home countdown

Pick your dates, download the widget, and drop it on your home screen. It counts the days on its own.

Android (4x2 widget)

  1. Tap Download widget. The file is saved as going-home-widget-4x2.html.
  2. Install an HTML widget app from the Play Store (for example “HTML Widget”).
  3. Long-press your home screen, add its widget, stretch it to 4x2, and choose the downloaded file.

iPhone

  1. Tap Download image and save it to Photos.
  2. Add a Photos widget (medium size, which is 4x2) and choose that picture.
Browsers can’t place a widget on a home screen by themselves, so the download plus the steps above is as close as a website gets. The image is a snapshot of today; download it again to refresh. The HTML widget updates itself.
